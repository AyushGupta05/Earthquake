### reading through it

Methodology - Take first 3 seconds p wave, measaure Pd (largest ground displacement). Bigger earthquake = Bigger Pd, correct for station to earthquake distnace 
log(Pd) = -3.463 + 0.729M - 1.374 log(R)
M = magnitude earthquake, R = distance
