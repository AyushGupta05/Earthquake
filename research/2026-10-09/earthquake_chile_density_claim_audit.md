# Claim audit: Chile likelihood floor and robust-density continuation

Checked 10 October 2026. Read-only research; no real-data arrays, checkpoints, Chile held-out outcomes, AWS jobs or production code were accessed. Existing completed INSTANCE report conclusions were read to avoid repeating rejected pilots. The frozen TRAIN diagnostic and running Chile baseline are unchanged.

**Assessment:** the diagnostic is scientifically useful, but a positive result alone supports a training-objective diagnosis. Removing the floor restores standard Gaussian-mixture maximum likelihood. The proposed moment-matched Huber head is an established density construction. A carefully controlled, transferable improvement could support an **EEW-specific empirical method**, but neither its statistics nor the currently proposed combination establishes priority. No clearly stronger mechanism is justified by the inspected source or literature; preserve the cheap diagnostic gate rather than add another architecture.

## Why this is more than floating-point arithmetic, but less than a new principle

Our algebraic interpretation is as follows. Write the TEAM mixture as p. A fixed-weight improper noise construction

\[
 \psi=\pi_0\delta+(1-\pi_0)p,
 \qquad \delta=\epsilon(1-\pi_0)/\pi_0
\]

gives `psi=(1-pi0)*(p+epsilon)`. Apart from a parameter-independent constant, its negative log objective equals TEAM's floor score. The total Gaussian pseudo-responsibility is `p/(p+epsilon)`, exactly its gradient attenuation. This is a **pointwise objective equivalence**, not a claim that TEAM implements RIMLE's fitted noise model, constraints or robustness theorem. The source does not establish that its authors intended the floor as an outlier model. The mapping shows why statistical consequences can survive perfectly stable arithmetic.

An additional elementary check: under an artificial affine target-coordinate change `m'=s*m+b`, s>0, the transformed density is `p'=p/s`. Keeping the same numerical epsilon gives `-log(p'+epsilon)=-log(p+s*epsilon)+log(s)`. Thus the effective suppression level depends on coordinate scale. Proper log likelihood changes only by log(s). This is a known density/Jacobian property and a potential unit test, **not** a physical mapping between MA, ML and Mw or a new magnitude-scaling law.

Robustness creates a genuine conflict here: discounting a badly modeled observation can protect against catalogue errors, but can also silence a valid scarce large event. The diagnostic cannot tell which interpretation is correct from the residual alone. Density-floor attenuation, component responsibility collapse, large predicted variance, head ReLU derivatives and global gradient clipping are separate mechanisms. The completed unblinded TRAIN checkpoint measures current suppression under that evaluation input policy; it cannot reconstruct suppression during earlier random cutoffs, label smoothing or station blinding.

Related neural optimization precedents strengthen that caution. [Seitzer et al., *On the Pitfalls of Heteroscedastic Uncertainty Estimation with Probabilistic Neural Networks* (ICLR 2022)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.09168) documents problematic likelihood optimization and variance-dependent reweighting; this primary abstract was verified. [Stirn et al., *Faithful Heteroscedastic Regression with Neural Networks* (AISTATS 2023)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v206/stirn23a.html) protects a mean estimator relative to its corresponding squared-error model. That guarantee does not transfer automatically to a mixture, median absolute error or rare-magnitude performance. These are relevant explanatory controls, not justification to launch additional methods now.

## What each possible outcome would support

1. **TRAIN gate negative:** no evidence of enough current floor suppression to justify this continuation. Stop under the existing protocol. No conclusion about historical suppression or all possible robust heads follows.
2. **Gate positive, proper Gaussian beats floored Gaussian:** a reproducible EEW baseline correction and objective-saturation analysis. Calling this simply a floating-point fix understates the statistical change; calling standard proper NLL a new method overstates it.
3. **Huber beats floored Gaussian but not proper Gaussian:** no evidence that the changed component family adds value. Retain the simpler correction.
4. **Huber repeatedly beats proper Gaussian and Gaussian-CRPS while preserving bulk mean/median errors:** potentially a useful EEW adaptation of an established robust density. To attribute the improvement to rare-event learning, retain exact parameter/moment, exposure, label-noise, cutoff, station-mask and optimizer controls; show event-level gains beyond one large earthquake; rule out mere interval widening; confirm on a separately frozen independent population. Until then it is an exploratory continuation result, not a general high-magnitude solution.

The existing INSTANCE proper-score and contextual-score grids failed the requested joint objective. Those findings justify restraint; they do not settle the Chile hypothesis because the former change a bounded residual head on a frozen representation, whereas this proposal changes the continuous end-to-end event objective. Conversely, a proper score is not an automatic solution: it targets the distribution induced by the sampling and label process, which here includes source magnitude oversampling and label smoothing. Neither proper NLL nor Huber restores a natural earthquake prior by itself.

**Recommendation:** keep the preregistered incidence gate and mandatory epsilon-zero control. Do not promote a robust head to the project's central contribution without matched evidence beyond the known score controls. If positive, the defensible framing is “detecting and mitigating inadvertent suppression of rare, valid magnitude observations in probabilistic EEW training,” supported by causal controls and external evidence—not a new probability law, new gradient identity, or new source physics.

## Search boundary

This bounded search covered EEW/magnitude with Huber, Student-t, robust likelihood, mixture-density training and CRPS; then the exact constant-density and normalized-Huber constructions. No exact published EEW implementation of this five-Huber continuation was located in the inspected primary sources. That is **not evidence of first use**. GaMMA's Huber arrival-time optimization is phase association/location, not a matching robust magnitude-density head; catalogue forecasting, aftershock b-value estimation, PGA classification and unrelated engineering losses were excluded. No unverified late-2026 results were used as performance evidence. This addendum does not change the original diagnostic thresholds, authorize fitting, or unseal held outcomes.
