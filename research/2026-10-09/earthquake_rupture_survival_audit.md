# Rupture survival: primary-source audit

A generic stop-versus-grow magnitude mixture has close earthquake prior art. Naming its components as rupture states does not identify those states from final magnitude labels. Two invented mixtures give the same tail probability 0.2 while their supposed growth probabilities are 0.2 and 0.5; their magnitude likelihoods are identical.

The audit also resolves earlier full-text access gaps for Wang 2022 and Morad 2025 through identified author manuscripts. Publisher PDFs and supplements remain explicit gaps. No regional first-network-P benchmark improvement follows from the teleseismic or laboratory results.

The 2022 rupture study already derives inverse upsampling of continuous probabilities under its own smooth sampler. That differs from Chile's banded repeat law and noisy targets, but further narrows any novelty claim for a prior correction.

No additional fit is justified. The useful next work remains the matched clock control and independent waveform feasibility, with all existing negative growth/censored/revision results retained.
