# TRAIN polarization diagnostic: feasibility and limitations

2026-10-09. No real waveform values or magnitude labels were inspected. The new TRAIN metadata audit, its six synthetic tests and isolated bundled Codex autoreview are complete. Extraction and fitting remain unstarted.

The schema genuinely supports a bounded diagnostic: the original counts HDF records advertise ENZ/channel×sample layout; eight explicitly TRAIN identities have int32[3,12000] arrays. Existing TRAIN-only cache shapes are979487×3×100/300/500. The3s file is named `Instance_windows_full.hdf5`. Cache shapes do not contain pre-P; pre-P must be read from the original named TRAIN counts traces.

The TRAIN header supplies integer manual P picks, interval and sample count, source depth, epicentral/hypocentral distance, identity and station epoch fields. All979487depth and distance entries are finite/nonnegative in the bounded metadata audit. The magnitude column was not loaded. Scalar responses, input units, azimuth/dip and epochs are present in the pinned StationXML archive. The final numeric coverage is in `metadata_audit.json`, including nonexclusive failures; waveform finiteness/variance, actual values and exhaustive HDF presence remain unchecked. No catalogue geometry or S-arrival time is proposed as an inference input.

These are **processed counts**, not common physical motion. Their format explicitly says response not restituted and velocity/acceleration mixed. Per-axis sensitivity division gives approximate native units; it neither removes the full response nor makes acceleration and velocity interchangeable. The three components must share a unit within each record. Canonical ENZ orientation is required; channel names alone do not prove it. Ambiguous, missing, noncanonical and epoch-crossing responses are excluded uniformly across controls. Review fixed two edge cases before final coverage: response overlap starting after trace start, and properties taken from a different epoch than the required window.

Version1 of the protocol preceded the metadata audit. Version2, agreed with the parent before waveform extraction or magnitude inspection, samples metadata-eligible rows before waveform quality checks and makes quality validity deadline-specific. This prevents a corrupt later suffix from changing the1s population. Sampling weights address sampling only, not waveform-quality nonresponse. The all-horizon intersection is descriptive, with horizon-specific population changes reported. The locked experimental gate, features, random-feature model and1800s CPU fit cap are unchanged.

Strict prefix tests concern the implemented operations on the released arrays. The original INSTANCE whole-record detrending/resampling remains a limitation; no raw-stream causality claim follows. Cross-component features could be especially sensitive to residual shared trends, so a positive diagnostic would still require a preprocessing robustness control before a physical interpretation.

The proposed probe tests **incremental information beyond85current descriptors**, not novelty of noise/polarization. [Böse, Heaton & Hauksson (2012)](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/kfqtk-94k74) already use a single3C sensor to estimate magnitude, distance and PGV, updating from0.25s; their reported site and extra-component gains are strong close prior art. [Zollo et al. (2023), §2.2](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1029/2022EA002657) explicitly compares currently observed displacement with pre-event displacement to select causal-filter quality settings, alongside probabilistic location. Those ideas cannot be claimed as new. Our immediate question is only whether the specified cross-component contrast survives matched marginal-noise controls and genuinely held events/stations in this data.

`feasibility_provenance.json` pins the audit, data-source hashes, tests, clean review and both protocol versions. Superseded audit/review files are retained as history and must not replace `metadata_audit.json` or the final review.
