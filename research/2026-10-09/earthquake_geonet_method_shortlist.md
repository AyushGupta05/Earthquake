# Two prospective GeoNet magnitude experiments

**The next method work will test a stronger spectral representation and explicit location uncertainty. Neither has demonstrated a gain or novelty.** These are modifications of published baselines under the common first-network-P clock, full Mw distribution, identical observations and matched point decisions.

First compare a small FME-inspired Fourier station encoder within TEAM-LM against the ordinary CNN and a fixed FFT-feature branch. Preserve prefix amplitude, flexible station attention and the same late instrument/site information in every arm. FME already establishes Fourier magnitude estimation; a gain must survive the fixed-feature control before attributing it to a learned spectral operator. [FME](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44195-026-00121-4), [TEAM-LM](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.02010).

Then test a TRAIN-supervised location distribution inferred from currently observed interstation P times and station coordinates. Integrate conditional magnitude densities across inferred source locations; compare with direct timing fusion, point geometry and expected log-distance controls that receive exactly the same observations and auxiliary supervision. With one arrival, location uncertainty should remain broad. Joint source uncertainty has existing Bayesian prior art; the prospective contribution is a useful neural coupling on independent events. [Virtual Seismologist author chapter](https://www.its.caltech.edu/~heatont/papers/VSmethod_cua_heaton%20%282%29.pdf), [probabilistic location method](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1029/2020JB020359).

An exact linear null prevents an easy false attribution: integrating a linear log-distance magnitude mean equals evaluating it at expected log distance. Beating a plug-in mean distance alone is a Jensen correction. Timing-only location weights also approximate total conditioning when waveforms inform location; the model is not an exact physical Bayes calculation.

Use a shared unbounded five-Gaussian Mw head, including the frame's largest Mw 8.0 cases, at 1/3/5 seconds. TRAIN-only sequence folds generate auxiliary predictions. No true catalogue distance, later P, future station selection or missing-pick silence enters deployment inputs. Tail error must improve at all deadlines while bulk MAE/MedAE and worst-error measures meet prospectively frozen tolerances. Proper density scores alone are insufficient.

The current 495-event metadata sample is a feasibility screen, with at most 169 candidate TEST events, before missingness. It is smaller than the proposed 200 available-TEST planning floor; a larger, separately frozen acquisition and final benchmark design remain necessary. No model fits or paid jobs have been launched for this shortlist.
