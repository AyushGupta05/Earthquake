# GeoNet method shortlist handoff

**Two prospective experiments, no demonstrated winner:** first test a stronger spectral representation; then test uncertain-distance coupling with cheap frozen-backbone heads. The full specification and primary links are in [SHORTLIST.md](PUBLIC_SHORTLIST.md). No implementation, fit, acquisition, reviewer submission or AWS action was performed.

| Candidate | Concrete implementation | Essential falsifier | Expected weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| FME-inspired station encoder within TEAM-LM | Two small Fourier/residual blocks; unchanged flexible station attention, late response/site features and five-Gaussian Mw head. Preserve prefix log-amplitude alongside normalized shape. | Matched CNN and CNN+fixed-FFT-feature controls, with identical information, clocks, loss, budget and nearly equal parameters. A fixed-feature tie rejects an operator-specific claim. | Short-prefix frequency ambiguity, sensor response/edge effects and station memorization. |
| Location-distribution coupling | TRAIN-supervised small location mixture from currently observed interstation P times/coordinates; infer a correlated station-distance vector per location sample; average conditional magnitude densities. | Direct timing/geometry-summary fusion, point geometry, and E[log R] controls all receive the same inputs and auxiliary information. A tie rejects an integration-specific claim. | One-second single-arrival geometry is broad; a location prior may mis-handle offshore/deep events; density gains may leave mean error unchanged. |

Use a TRAIN-internal sequence-held pilot first, with all 1/3/5 horizons selected jointly. Candidate1 needs three jointly trained arms and two seeds for replication, rather than separate per-horizon models. Candidate2 first freezes the prespecified backbone, generates two-fold out-of-fold TRAIN location predictions and fits small shared conditional heads. TRAIN catalogue geometry is an auxiliary target/offline audit only; it never substitutes for inferred distance at deployment. Learn the one-arrival geometry prior from TRAIN cases. Do not use later P times, future station selection or missing-pick silence.

For a linear conditional mean in log distance, integrating location uncertainty gives the same mean as plugging E[log R]. The invented R=10/100 equal-weight example gives5.5 for both, whereas plugging E[R]=55 gives5.74036. A win against E[R] alone is not an uncertainty-integration result. Timing-only geometry weights also approximate total conditioning when waveforms inform location; no exact Bayes claim is made.

Require tail-MAE gains at every deadline while preserving event MAE/MedAE and CVaR95 under TRAIN-frozen tolerances and matched point decisions. Report proper scores without allowing them to substitute for error reduction. Use adequate unbounded Mw support through8.0. FME/TEAM-LM already establish the encoder/density ingredients; VS/Zollo establish joint source uncertainty. The prospective contribution is a specific effective neural coupling, if its controls lose on independent events, rather than generic distribution estimation.

Independent sequence isolation and available-prefix counts remain unresolved; exact physical calibration is not a prerequisite for ordinary counts-based engineering controls.
