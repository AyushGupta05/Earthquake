# Frozen scalar confound preflight

Frozen before arithmetic on 11 October 2026. This is a source-led, train-data-free identifiability check, not a seismic simulation, learned-model test or acquisition authorization. No panel is selected by a favorable result. Retain every calculation. All numerical values below are invented.

The candidate is the already-shortlisted FME-inspired station encoder. The only defensible residual hypothesis is finite-capacity representation of **available phase-sensitive low-frequency shape**, alongside preserved amplitude and identical late metadata. This is not a new observation: a raw-prefix CNN already receives that shape. The calculations can expose misleading interpretations but cannot rank learned encoders or establish a high-Mw benefit.

## Fixed panels

1. **Amplitude compression.** For a nonconstant linear feature with population variance 1, calculate the InstanceNorm scale factor `c/sqrt(c*c + eps)` at `eps=1e-5`, `c in {1,10,100}`. With `eps=0`, positive scaling is exactly removed after subtracting a constant bias; with positive epsilon it is approximately removed. Compare the retained side feature `log10(c)`; do not label this a natural larger-earthquake simulation.
2. **Station order.** For two invented station features `h=(1,3)`, an order-sensitive flattened readout has weights `(1,2)`. Compare both permutations, and the symmetric sum control. This shows that such a model class need not be invariant; it is not an execution or empirical diagnosis of the FME checkpoint.
3. **Mode index versus physical frequency.** At `fs=100`, first32 modes means `k=0..31`. Calculate the highest retained Hz for lengths `{600,800,1000}`, corresponding to a five-second preamble plus `{1,3,5}` seconds, and for a common zero-padded length1000. Padding changes the coefficient grid, not the observed signal duration or information. Also record the two-tap positive average's circular response to an endpoint impulse algebraically; no learned operator is executed.
4. **Known frequency-estimator noise bias.** Use a deliberately simple RMS-frequency period `tau=sqrt(sum(P)/sum(f*f*P))`, not the specific formulas of Ziv or Wolfe. Signal power is1 at0.5Hz; add noise power0.01 at20Hz. Compare clean, contaminated and ideal known-noise removal. The latter is an oracle. A fixed FFT summary sees the same bins; improvements here cannot identify an operator-specific mechanism. This toy contains no earthquake labels.
5. **Unresolved source-spectrum ambiguity.** For the invented *complete-source* Brune-shaped family `D(f)=Omega/(1+(f/fc)^2)`, hold geometry/radiation/attenuation fixed. Compare `Omega1=1, fc1=.1` with `Omega2=10^1.5, fc2=.1/sqrt(10^1.5)` at frequencies `{1,3,5}`Hz. The plateau ratio represents a one-unit Mw difference under a fixed moment conversion; the stress-drop relation is not held constant. Calculate their amplitude ratios and the common high-frequency product `Omega*fc^2`. These are not Fourier coefficients of truncated P waves. Small high-frequency differences do not imply exact nonidentifiability with noiseless data; the example diagnoses ill-conditioning and nuisance sensitivity.

## Algebra-only controls, with no fit

- For real `x` of lengthN, reversal has DFT `X_rev[k]=exp(-2*pi*i*k*(N-1)/N)*conj(X[k])`. Thus complete FFT powers, peak and RMS coincide, but complex phase and raw time order can differ. Four equal-energy blocks whose internal samples are reversed also leave quarter RMS unchanged. No magnitude is assigned to either ordering: that would invent the physical association being tested.
- An invertible DFT of the same complete available prefix adds no information conditional on that raw prefix. A lossy power summary can discard phase. Beating a power-summary model therefore does not identify new observations or prove superiority to a matched raw-waveform model.
- Global terminal inference over an available prefix is causal with respect to the forecast deadline. The same transform is not causal for earlier internal timestamps unless their observations are separately masked. Changing a future suffix and adding a station whose pick occurs after the deadline must leave the earlier input exactly unchanged.

## Interpretation rule

These panels are guards, not an MAE gate. They earn **zero learned fits** by themselves. No bulk/tail metric, synthetic magnitude accuracy, capacity comparison or station/response transfer is estimated. A later engineering test must distinguish power/phase, effective receptive field, amplitude bypass, station order, preprocessing and capacity using identical observations and a matched density head. Do not turn a failure of a deliberately lossy summary into evidence that a Fourier module predicts an unresolved rupture endpoint.
