# Spectral encoder mechanism audit

11 October 2026. Source/literature and invented scalar arithmetic only. No real rows, prediction arrays, weights or response inventories were inspected; no model was executed, data acquired, fit launched or AWS resource changed. One overbroad lexical search surfaced incidental notebook output in truncated tool text; it was not analyzed, copied here or used as evidence. Subsequent reads were confined to named sources/reports. Existing protocols and reports remain unchanged.

**Decision: retain the spectral encoder as an ordinary engineering comparator; no distinct spectral high-Mw correction is justified by this investigation.** The surviving hypothesis is better finite-capacity use of phase-sensitive low-frequency shape already present in the available raw prefix. Published spectral, amplitude-preserving and dynamic-station methods occupy its ingredients. A positive result could establish a useful adaptation after matched controls; the sources and scalar checks establish neither novelty nor accuracy. They earn zero learned fits by themselves.

## What the FME source actually implements

Read only the already-cached [author model source at commit04d1c30](https://github.com/whywhy21/FME/blob/04d1c30cb0c7ea319b37c1ad63e00fa949063767/FME_tools/model.py),SHA256`9fefa8bb521b7c93386f138b9178092685c9377a18faba773b7c98016b78f5c5`. `FME_fr` has separate Fourier stacks for10 station slots, internal affine InstanceNorm, distance multiplication, then a bidirectional LSTM receiving `(batch,stations,200)`, followed by flattening/scalar FC. Thus that example's LSTM sequence axis is **station**, and station permutation augmentation is not structural invariance. The first spectral branch has a nominal0–5Hz range plus a pointwise path. These are inspected-source facts, not proof that every published experiment used this exact example. No code/weight was executed; no license decision or new download was made.

Agent-derived consequences: for `z=Lx+b` with constant bias, population normalization gives `(cLx−mean(cLx))/sqrt(c²var(Lx)+epsilon)`. Positive gain is compressed; epsilon prevents exact invariance. Retain the identical log-amplitude bypass in all arms; do not reopen failed gain augmentation.

Station order, distance and amplitude handling can explain gains independently of spectral inference. Pointwise/nonlinear paths can retain/regenerate high frequencies; spectral truncation is not proof of whole-model physical denoising.

## Sharpened, falsifiable mechanism

**H:** given identical prefix samples, amplitude bypass, current station set and metadata, a phase-preserving spectral mixer learns useful long-period temporal arrangement more efficiently than the chosen CNN within the same fit budget. That arrangement might reduce underestimation in some large events. H is an engineering induction/optimization hypothesis. It neither adds information nor identifies final rupture duration or corner frequency. The accessed sources establish no mechanism guaranteeing large-Mw improvement while retaining bulk/worst performance.

The phase-sensitive arrangement is the only observable distinction from *power-only summaries*. It is not a distinction from raw waveforms. It also must not be renamed rupture growth, posterior revision, entropy, censored peak innovations or response invariance: those earlier pilots are negative and are retained. Site/response metadata supply nuisance context; they do not make early spectral shape a uniquely identified source quantity. A late-site gain with identical encoders is already an established explanation.

For a future **single** spectral comparison, retain the prespecified CNN and the FME-inspired shared station encoder with the same five-Gaussian unbounded Mw head. The CNN+fixedFFT arm needs a precise statement of its transform. Power-only coefficients can test summary utility; beating them cannot isolate Fourier mixing from phase retention. If H is the claim, give this existing control fixed **complex** prefix coefficients (or another phase-preserving invertible view), with the same physical frequency axis/mask, log-amplitude and metadata. A CNN/global-temporal control must have comparable effective receptive field and±5% parameter count. No extra architecture grid is proposed; the final comparison can be frozen before any future fit, and this audit does not change the old shortlist in place.
