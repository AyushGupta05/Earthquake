# MAGNET paper capsule

Berman, Zlydenko, Gilon, Matias and Bar-Sinai, *Earthquake magnitudes depend on seismic history, as revealed by a neural network analysis*, Scientific Reports 16, 21076 (7 May 2026), DOI 10.1038/s41598-026-48563-7. MAGNET combines recurrent history encoders with the queried event's location and time, producing two stretched Kumaraswamy components. It separates training, validation and test chronologically and evaluates likelihood above estimated completeness thresholds to address short-time incompleteness. The paper reports approximately 0.07 bit average gain over GR across three regions. A spatially varying GR control is competitive in New Zealand; some major-event aftermaths show losses. This is catalog-conditioned density evidence, not a matched waveform EEW result at 1/3/5 seconds, nor evidence that both mean/median actions or rare-event absolute errors improve. The supplied query coordinates need special treatment in an EEW adaptation. [Final journal paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-48563-7), [arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.02129v2).

This is the only detailed paper capsule in this packet. Subsequent equations, prerequisites and control proposals are this project's analysis, not additional reported results.
