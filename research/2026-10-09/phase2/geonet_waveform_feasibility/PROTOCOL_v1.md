# Small GeoNet waveform and response feasibility audit

Frozen before any StationXML or miniSEED acquisition, 11 October 2026 (London). Local CPU only. No AWS resource, fitting, encoder preprocessing, Chile TEST reads or larger acquisition. This is a six-event sample audit, not a benchmark or operational picker replay.

## Fixed metadata inputs and deterministic sample

Use only the completed census at sibling geonet_tail_census/local_runs/recovery_20261010. Verify the exact manifest, groups, outcomes, query_order and summary hashes in census_input_pins.json before selection. Its 149 unique Mw≥5.5 IDs passed the frozen census rules; no unknown-ID/time join or newer source substitution is allowed.

Select at most two IDs in each nonoverlapping Mw band [5.5,6), [6,7), [7,infinity). Require eligible groups with min/max Mw wholly within a band and successful census outcomes. In each band, select one ID from its earliest calendar year and one from its latest calendar year. Order within each chosen year by SHA256 of geonet-small-waveform-v1|EVENT_ID, then ID. If the years coincide, choose the first two in that order. No replacement or enlargement based on waveform/response outcomes.

For each selected ID, define T0 as its earliest accepted NZ-associated P pick, exactly as in the census. Collapse NZ stations to their earliest accepted pick, tie by (location,channel,pick_id), rank by observed time then station code, and take the first three. Freeze the observed location and first two characters of its three-character channel as a literal prefix. Invalid station/location/channel syntax yields an explicit unqueryable target without replacement. Requests use that frozen location and prefix?, not a metadata-informed search for a better sensor. Do not interpret T0 as packet receipt or a causal trigger.

Prepare the complete deterministic request list and SHA256 under ignored local_runs before acquisition: one HTTPS archive response-level StationXML query followed by one miniSEED dataselect query per queryable target. Both cover [T0-5s,T0+5s], same network/station/location/prefix. Empty SEED location is requested as --. Maximum 6 events, 18 stations and 36 planned queries. Raw input-derived IDs, station identities, URLs and picks stay local; publish only sample counts/bands/year range and list hashes. Only the parent may create an acquisition receipt after clean code-only review and pre-acquisition Git commit/push. The receipt must bind source/protocol/input/plan/dependency hashes and the Git commit. Preparation or this protocol alone cannot launch acquisition.

## Absolute prefix availability

For each target, evaluate [T0-5s,T0+t) for t=1,3,5 seconds. GeoNet may return samples outside the requested interval, and ObsPy endpoint trimming can include boundaries. Therefore crop by exact rational sample indices from absolute nanosecond start times and recorded sample rates, with strictly no sample at/after T0+t. Do not filter, demean, taper, resample, merge, interpolate, pad, remove response or use a future suffix for any summary. Samples exactly at the deadline are excluded. An annotated station P pick at/after a deadline is separately marked as not yet observed by that retrospective clock.

For every prefix, require one coherent ENZ or 12Z triplet at the frozen location/prefix (ENZ has fixed precedence), positive finite common sample rate, one uniquely matching response epoch per component spanning the whole prefix, valid orientation (vertical dip within 10 degrees of vertical, horizontal dips within 10 degrees, horizontal azimuth difference 90±10 degrees), a positive finite instrument sensitivity and frequency, explicit physical input units M, M/S or M/S**2 and count output units, response stages, and a finite nonzero evalresp transfer at the stated sensitivity frequency. Preserve response warnings/errors. This is response-metadata consistency, not calibration against an independent instrument.

Keep miniSEED trace pieces separate. Accept timing-rounding discrepancies of at most min(1 microsecond,0.01 sample period); a missing whole sample, overlap or wrong sample rate fails the prefix. The first retained sample must occur within one sample interval of the left bound and the last within one sample interval of the strict deadline. Require consistent within-component and cross-component sample lattices; equal rates alone do not establish alignment. No trace piece outside the prefix may influence these results. Require finite samples. Report flatline and repeated prefix-extreme runs of at least eight samples as clipping candidates, preserving a run across contiguous same-rate pieces and resetting for gaps/overlaps/nonfinite samples. A clean screen cannot prove physical absence of clipping or the ADC full-scale limit. Report timing-quality/calibration metadata when present, preserving missing values. No clean-screen result may be named verified unclipped.

## Resource limits and missingness

One serial local CPU run. A 1800-second outer deadline starts before parent validation/imports and covers loading, all requests, decoding and audit; bounded local validation reads reject inputs above 32MiB each (plan/receipt 1MiB). A monotonic downloader deadline also stops requests, and each request has at most 20 seconds. At most 100 attempts and 128 MiB response bodies combined across this audit; protocol/source/dependency browsing is outside that data-transfer accounting and separately recorded. No retry, redirect, replacement or automatic resume. Requests begin at least one second apart. Per-response caps: StationXML4MiB, miniSEED8MiB. Every attempt and received partial body counts. Reject DTD/entity/NUL StationXML and miniSEED declaring more than 64MiB of decoded sample memory. If StationXML retrieval fails, its paired waveform request is explicitly unattempted; no substitute station. If decoding/response validation fails after retrieval, preserve it and continue the frozen remaining targets. A cap, child failure or process timeout yields incomplete, never a negative scientific result or permission to extend the run. Keep all frozen station/deadline denominators even if interrupted. Capture the worker's native stderr (including evalresp warnings) in an ignored local log; retain Python warnings per response as well.

Every event/station/deadline retains its denominator and reason: failed HTTP, bad XML/miniSEED, missing triplet/epoch/units/stages, ambiguous epoch, gaps/overlaps/nonfinite samples, clipping candidates, or unattempted/capped. Publish aggregate feasibility counts and hash receipts only after independent verification. Raw miniSEED, StationXML, URLs/IDs and per-target rows remain under ignored local_runs and out of Git/review. No training follows from this audit alone.
