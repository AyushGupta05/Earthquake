# TT-SAM prefix-source audit

**Published-run prefix causality: unknown. Current public demo processing order: verified.** The inspected source does not establish preprocessing from only the available prefix. Later tensor zeroing establishes retained support, not independence of retained values from an unseen suffix. This is neither a leakage finding nor evidence of magnitude improvement.

The [official article](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1029/2025JH001005), published 29 April 2026, predicts PGV and assesses CWA intensity on 2016 test events. The full model’s 97.8% is within ±1 intensity level at 13 s after first-trigger P arrival; 52.8% matches exactly. Comparison forecast optima are 7/7/13 s. Inputs include 0.33 Hz low-frequency velocity and Pd/CVAV/TP time series; training uses random 3–15 s suffix cuts. The paper cites [software archive 18923222](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18923222). No separate supplement link surfaced in the accessible HTML. These results do not measure 1/3/5 s magnitude accuracy, and forecast offsets are not end-to-end operational latency.

The [author repository](https://github.com/YuHengChen54/TT-SAM) identifies itself as the paper’s implementation. Four inert source blobs were inspected statically; none was imported or executed. Tree membership and exact blob bytes are pinned in `SOURCE_PINS.json`. The table concerns this public snapshot, not a certified reproduction of the publication.

The narrow next evidence needed is the exact published source/configuration and dependency pins, plus a source-only demonstration of prefix equivalence. It should compare invented signals with identical observed prefixes and different unseen tails, and compare full-preprocessed retained prefixes against preprocessing of the prefixes alone. Check every waveform/feature channel, transformation state, normalization and trigger clock at the proposed 1/3/5 s cuts and the training cuts. Specify tolerance before testing and retain all mismatches. No fit, actual waveform acquisition, author contact or superiority/novelty claim is earned by this audit. Our magnitude target requires its own magnitude labels, metrics and information clock; CWA intensity/PGV results cannot be relabelled as Mw accuracy.
