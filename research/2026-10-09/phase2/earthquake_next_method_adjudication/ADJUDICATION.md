# One conditional next magnitude experiment

11 October 2026. Documentation/primary-paper adjudication only. No code, private inputs/data, predictions, model, fit, AWS, acquisition, installation, Git, or existing-freeze modification.

**Decision: no GPU fit is earned now.** The single best conditional experiment is **real-event, Mw-compatible cross-region transfer in the existing unbounded magnitude-density model, with target bulk replay**. It has more direct published evidence for reducing large-event underestimation than the surviving history-prior or spectral proposals. It is established transfer learning, not a novelty claim, and no all-1/3/5-second GeoNet improvement follows from that paper.

TEAM-LM's joint regional training/transfer reduced large-event saturation and RMSE in Chile and Italy; Japan gained little when it already supplied most large examples. Target fine-tuning could degrade Italy's largest-event predictions. Its labels were Chile **MA**, Italy predominantly **ML** with some Mw/mb, and Japan **MJMA**. Its evaluation clock was first network P, at **0.5/1/2/4/8/16/25 seconds**, rather than our exact 1/3/5 grid. These are direct published findings; our proposed Mw-compatible donor restriction, bulk replay and acceptance gate are new experimental choices, not reported results. [TEAM-LM v2, §§2.1, 3.1, 3.3, 4.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2101.02010v2).

## Hypothesis and matched experiment

**H:** adding distinct, genuinely recorded TRAIN events with compatible Mw≥5.5 labels improves the learned early waveform-to-Mw density beyond additional exposure to existing rare examples or ordinary foreign waveforms; its fixed mean action reduces GeoNet Mw≥5.5 event error at **each** of 1/3/5 seconds while preserving bulk and worst errors. Added waveform diversity/label coverage is the proposed mechanism. It is neither a posthoc tail lift nor a latent rupture-state interpretation.

Keep one unchanged TEAM-style encoder, amplitude bypass, station/metadata inputs, and continuous five-Gaussian Mw head in every arm. Means remain uncapped and scales positive, so Mw8 is not outside categorical support. Use the same target TRAIN/calibration splits, proper density objective, two fixed seeds, common target replay batches, total updates, preprocessing, final checkpoint rule and tuning budget. Replace only the non-target-replay batches:

| Arm | Additional batches / matched question |
|---|---|
| A: target only | More ordinary target TRAIN batches. Does transfer beat equal compute and target exposure? |
| B: repeated target tail | Reuse target TRAIN tail events to match rare-band exposure where those bands exist. Does new real-event information beat repeating known examples? |
| C: foreign ordinary events | Distinct lower-Mw donor events from the same donor regions/instrument strata and count budget. Is the gain merely extra regional/path/site diversity? |
| D: candidate | Distinct Mw≥5.5 donor events plus the same target bulk replay. |

D must beat A/B/C on the predeclared objective. Match magnitude-band exposure for B only where actual target labels support it; never invent higher labels. If D extends a magnitude range absent from target TRAIN, extra label-range coverage and event diversity cannot be isolated there: report that combined mechanism. Do not claim this experiment isolates physical rupture predictability. Keep sampling/weights explicit and calibration assessed on the target population; a proper loss under a changed sampling population is not an automatic target-posterior guarantee. No new prior-ratio correction, growth head, score grid or response architecture is bundled into D.

The old rare-exposure pilot is a retained negative: repeating/reweighting current sparse labels and correcting the exposure prior failed all 12 fixed comparisons. D would add independently recorded examples and therefore asks a different question, while B remains an established matched control. ## Proposed success/failure and investment gate

These are **new prospective proposed criteria**, not edits to any old freeze. Before any actual input or fit, adopt/freeze the cohort, sequence/station partitions, donor eligibility, support, budgets, action and exact simultaneous paired-interval procedure. Use a separate sealed evaluation and a fixed denominator retaining failures/unknowns; sequence/time-block dependence must be audited. Existing 200 usable/30 Mw≥5.5/15 Mw≥6 planning floors are support floors, not power guarantees; Mw≥7 remains descriptive unless independently supported. [Next-data planning boundary](/Users/ayush/Documents/Codex/2026-10-09/ok-x20/outputs/earthquake_geonet_next_data_plan.md).

Proposed primary gate: at every horizon, D's **mean-action event-macro Mw≥5.5 MAE** improves by at least **0.02 magnitude** over each comparator; both seeds have the favorable effect. Evaluate each seed separately and average its scalar metrics for the paired interval analysis; do not switch to an ensemble prediction. Simultaneous one-sided 95% sequence/block paired intervals must support that improvement and **zero-margin** nondegradation of bulk MAE, MedAE, CVaR95, NLL and CRPS. Here MedAE is median absolute error of the fixed mean action; bulk means every event in the fixed target evaluation cohort. Report P95 and maximum error; any observed worst-error worsening in either seed vetoes promotion. Fix the family/error control before results, and report distribution-median actions secondarily without switching the chosen action. No unsupported tail or interval is called a pass. An interval crossing the boundary is inconclusive, not a favorable result. Any failed horizon, seed, bulk guard, integrity/clock/support gate, or matched-control contrast means no promotion; retain all arms and failures.

**Minimal evidence before a GPU fit:** an authorized, frozen source/label/clock manifest for compatible independent Mw donors; enough usable target bulk and independent tail sequences for the chosen uncertainty claim; no donor/target evaluation or sequence contamination; common causal preprocessing/units/support; a reviewed bounded implementation with invented tests and unchanged baselines; and separate input/resource authorization. None is established by this document. The current 495-event metadata screen offers at most 169 candidate TEST events before attrition, below the proposed 200 planning floor, and the 96-event waveform screen remains incomplete. Resolving those support/provenance gaps is useful work; another fit on reused sparse validation is not earned.
