[P2] [P2] Retain wrong-root channel responses as target failures
auditor.py:314
A bounded, pinned HTTP-200 body containing well-formed XML with a different root, such as `<html/>`, raises AuditError here. qualification_batch explicitly rethrows AuditError, so this ordinary metadata-parser failure aborts the entire replay instead of retaining the affected target's failure and continuing with independent targets. Syntactically malformed XML already follows the ordinary-failure path. Distinguish invalid retrieved metadata from provenance, bounds, and resource interruptions.

auditor.py:900
qualification_batch records waveform_header_status, waveform_decoder_status, and channel_parser_status, but this aggregation loop omits all three. Their failures otherwise collapse into unresolved_engineering, while raw reason counters are deliberately excluded from public summaries. Consequently, the public aggregate cannot distinguish successful retrieval followed by header, decoding, or channel-parsing failure as required by the replay checklist. Publish stable counters for these stages, with explicit failed states where appropriate.

[P2] [P2] Reject requests recorded after the completed launch ended
auditor.py:283
The launch-duration check only verifies that outer_elapsed_seconds is within 7200 seconds; it never checks that every request ended before that duration. For example, a request starting at 100 seconds with elapsed_seconds=1 passes alongside outer_elapsed_seconds=5, assuming the other pins match. Those records cannot describe a completed parent/child launch sharing the stated clock. Require the recorded launch duration to encompass every attempt's start plus elapsed time before accepting completion.
