## State and duplicate limits

The authored causal-response audit gives the relevant distinction: available pre-P samples may constrain inherited response state, but need not be stationary noise. In an invented scalar response `y0=0.9s+u0`, observed `y0=2.8` is compatible with `(s,u0)=(2,1)` or `(0,2.8)`. A complete shorter grid cannot choose between them; setting missing state to zero is an extra assumption. Five seconds itself is not proof of adequate state initialization. Full response, orientation, physical units and causal receipt remain unverified.

Strict append suffices only for observed pieces that are disjoint, contiguous and rate/lattice compatible within the required window. It cannot resolve duplicate over-delivery. An invented 200 Hz pair samples times −10,−5,0 ms with values 1,2,3 and times 0,5,10 ms with values 3,4,5. Its identical time-zero sample is duplicated; the unchanged core correctly rejects the appended overlap. Equal amplitudes alone are not duplicate proof. Exact NSLC/rate/rational-time/value agreement, retained dtype and provenance plus timing-quality/calibration treatment would be required for a separate duplicate certificate. Conflicting or merely nearby samples cannot be averaged, rounded, chosen preferentially or erased. This protocol adds no object, deduplicates nothing and retains any overlap as failure/ambiguity. Record over-delivery documented for FDSN does not prove cross-day duplication in this archive.
