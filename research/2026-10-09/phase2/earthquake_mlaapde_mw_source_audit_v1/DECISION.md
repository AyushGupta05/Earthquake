## What the separate official schemas support

The USGS **GeoJSON Detail** schema explicitly includes `mag`, `magType`, `id`, `ids`, `updated` and source products with `source`, `type`, `code`, `updateTime`, `preferredWeight` and content descriptors. This supplies a possible catalogue identity/type/version representation. It does **not** establish MLAAPDE's join keys or original catalogue snapshot, nor identify the magnitude author merely from a product contributor. The current ComCat overview was also inspected; its overview text is not a metadata dictionary. No actual catalogue query was made. [Official schema](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/feed/v1.0/geojson_detail.php).

USGS describes Mww/Mwc/Mwb/Mwr as distinct moment-tensor magnitude methods with different useful ranges; Mwp is an integrated-P estimate, and mb/ML/Ms remain separate scales. A proposed eligibility policy must name the accepted methods and provenance, rather than accept a preferred numeric magnitude or every `mw*` string. Compatibility with GeoNet CMT Mw, uncertainty and range-dependent biases remains an audit requirement. This is a suggested policy boundary, not a released MLAAPDE field mapping. [Official magnitude-type definitions, current search-indexed text](https://www.usgs.gov/programs/earthquake-hazards/magnitude-types).

FDSN's response documentation describes stage transfer functions/gains, input/output units, sensitivity at a specified frequency and digitizer decimation/delay/correction. These distinguish a frequency-dependent response from one scalar count-to-unit factor. They do **not** show that MLAAPDE bundles any such inventory. [Official StationXML response documentation and metadata examples](https://docs.fdsn.org/projects/stationxml/en/latest/response.html).

## Exact prerequisites for the conditional route

These are semantic requirements, **not invented MLAAPDE column names**:

1. First obtain an accessible official, immutable release schema/configuration/export mapping. It must establish stable event/provider identifiers, magnitude value/type/method/author/uncertainty and catalogue revision, or an exact reproducible join to a separately pinned catalogue. Retain unresolved aliases, duplicates, missing types and unknown values. A current preferred catalogue value must not silently replace a historical label. Moment-tensor provenance supplies a retrospective target; it never becomes an inference input.
2. Establish event-to-trace-to-pick correspondence: literal network/station/location/channel identity, phase/status/use eligibility, absolute observed pick time, absolute trace start, exact sample rate/count, component ordering/orientation and declared preprocessing. A relative P sample offset is usable only if its absolute-time relation is documented. Phase-centred excerpts and source origin times do not by themselves establish the network T0.
3. Reconstruct the same **declared accepted-P subset and station-availability rule** as the target experiment, with T0 from that subset and samples limited to [T0−5,T0+h), h=1/3/5. Report incomplete-reference/coverage limits. Do not claim the subset is the earliest complete physical network, operational picker or packet-reception clock. P/Pn/Pg/S/Sn/Sg availability in an archive is not an online station-arrival manifest; late stations need their actual shorter post-P age.
4. Establish concurrent epoch/response joins and original acquisition/processing provenance. Raw counts are not calibrated velocity/acceleration and do not prove an unfiltered original-rate input. A sensitivity-only path is a separately labelled approximation; physical comparability needs the appropriate full transfer chain and a frozen prefix-only correction/filter/resampling contract with explicit delay/state/boundaries. Full-window normalization or suffix-dependent zero-phase processing is inadmissible.
5. Only after those source prerequisites, freeze distinct ordinary/≥5.5/≥6/≥7 genuine Mw event and independent-sequence counts, regions/instrument strata, and donor/target overlap exclusions. No such counts or usable cohorts were established here. Preserve the predeclared A/B/C/D equal-compute controls and all-horizon/bulk/worst-error gates; transfer is not novel and is not qualified by a schema alone.
