# Next GeoNet data step: a prospective design

**Prefer a direct 96-event raw-count waveform screen to the draft response-only screen.** Response-only metadata cannot establish sample continuity, three-component availability, clipping flags or a usable early prediction input. The next screen should resolve those engineering unknowns while retaining unavailable responses as missing information. This document is a design, not a source/target freeze, acquisition receipt or permission to launch. No requests, implementation, fits, reviewer submission, AWS or shared edits occurred.

## Preserve the existing phase

The current 346 lower-M metadata phase remains unchanged: its 15 strata, exact sample hashes, 149 cached tails, 312-origin / 277-NZ-P / per-stratum gates, 400-attempt / 128-MiB / 1,800-second limits and no retry/replacement/resume rules still apply. Its published preparation report says collection is disabled pending exact runtime/cached-base equality; no result is invented here. Finish and independently replay that phase before preparing the successor. Failure does not retrospectively lower its thresholds or expand its caps. [Frozen metadata protocol](../geonet_bulk_metadata/PROTOCOL_v1.md), [current report](../../outputs/earthquake_geonet_bulk_metadata.md).

Cached tail support is useful but sparse: all 149 have an accepted NZ P, while ≥3 currently accepted stations at 1/3/5 s occur in 23/57/82 of 149 Mw≥5.5 events, 10/22/30 of 60 Mw≥6 and 2/6/7 of 15 Mw≥7. Counts are lower bounds at the **fixed accepted-subset T0**, not an earlier hypothetical complete-pick clock. Twenty-two events have 707 unresolved references; do not fill them with theoretical P times. All 149 have valid in-window origin time/geometry according to the parent's independent cached audit. Requiring three early stations would select away most one-second large events. [Census](../geonet_tail_census/RESULTS.md).

The completed six-event waveform audit has 15/18 strict 3C + screen targets at each horizon, with P intersections 9/11/13. Two response queries returned 204 and their paired waveforms were unattempted, so that audit cannot say those waveforms are absent. Its earliest/latest-year stress selection is not representative. [Verified waveform results](../geonet_waveform_feasibility/RESULTS.md).

## Stage 1: direct 96 counts engineering screen

Retain the draft event allocation: all 60 pinned Mw≥6 events, two lower-M events in each of 15 calendar/band cells (30), and two [5.5,6) events per calendar partition (6). Maximum 96 events. Rank the lower events **within the existing frozen 346 selected IDs**, before conditioning on their metadata outcome, by SHA256 of `geonet-counts-screen-v1|PARTITION|BAND|EVENT_ID`; use the same key for the six additional tail events from their pinned band/calendar cells. Tie by ID. Failure/unknown/no-P selections remain unreplaced, with zero queryable targets and their original event denominator. No IDs are computed or published here.

At each global deadline, use **all currently accepted arrived stations up to cap 8**, ranked by accepted arrival time then station identity. Collapse each station to its earliest pick with the existing tie rules. An input station must have accepted P strictly before T0+t. Do not require three stations. Do not replace a selected failed waveform with station 9. The union of these selections through +5 s determines acquisition bookkeeping only; no later station identity, later pick, eventual station count or future selection mask reaches 1/3 s inputs.

Anchor each station's sensor to the observed pick's location/prefix and uniquely valid concurrent channel metadata. A predeclared ENZ-before-12Z rule can resolve a coherent triplet within that family; do not choose a different family after seeing samples. Channel epoch/orientation/family selection must hold throughout each available prefix, including its prebuffer. Assess each prefix separately; a later epoch change cannot disqualify an earlier valid prefix. Missing or invalid channel identity/epoch is unresolved; missing **full transfer response** can still permit clearly labelled native-count engineering. There is no claim of calibrated velocity/acceleration, rotation or full deconvolution from counts alone.

Freeze each waveform query envelope from the accepted pick station, location and two-character channel prefix, with the component wildcard, before seeing the channel-query outcome. Acquire `[T0−5s,T0+5s]` once per selected target. Qualify the triplet against concurrent channel metadata afterward; waveform values never decide the family. Retrieve channel-level metadata and batched miniSEED independently: a failed response/channel request does **not** suppress the frozen waveform attempt. A waveform without valid channel metadata remains a retained unresolved engineering target, rather than nonexistent data. Reuse exactly matching cached bytes after hash/epoch/window validation. Batch up to 16 events per waveform POST; never request continuous days merely to extract 10-second prefixes. No station or event replacements, retries, redirects or automatic resume.

Evaluate `[T0−5s,T0+t)` with exact absolute sample indices. Preserve sample rate/lattice, gaps/overlaps, finite values, left-edge coverage and orientation/epoch reasons. Do not merge/interpolate, shift T0, zero-fill missing data, or preprocess using a future suffix. Clipping/flatline candidates remain flags and denominators; a clean screen is not verified unclipped physics. Publish count-valid and full-response status separately. Channel-level requests do not establish a full transfer chain; a stronger response-consistent panel uses only cached or separately acquired valid full-response evidence. Full-response success is not a gate for count-valid engineering.

Freeze all 96 event×3-deadline outcomes and all selected station slots, including unknown/unattempted. An engineering-available event has ≥1 selected valid 3C count prefix with accepted P before that deadline. Primary available-cohort support is the intersection across all three horizons; also report horizon-specific coverage. Denominators include every selected event, not just queried ones. Report band/calendar and response-family missingness without inverse-response weighting.

Suggested **successor allocations only**, not operative caps: at most 768 station targets, 768 metadata plus 6 waveform POSTs before cache reuse, 800 attempts, 512 MiB combined bodies, 4 MiB/metadata response, 16 MiB/waveform batch and a 7,200-second outer deadline covering preparation/loading/requests/decoding. Keep 20-second requests and ≥1-second request starts; cap decoded memory separately. If a cap or deadline stops the run, retain unattempted slots and mark the receipt incomplete; those slots are not observed waveform failures. A separately frozen implementation must validate these numbers and bind runtime/source/targets before requests.

This screen decides whether a larger raw-count cohort is worth acquiring. It does not earn fitting or a response-only availability claim. In particular its 31 candidate TEST events are at most 19 Mw≥6, two [5.5,6) and 10 lower-M; it cannot meet 200 TEST or 30 TEST-tail planning floors. A poor first-three-station result alone cannot condemn cap 8 input availability; that is why the draft uses the actual variable-station contract.

## Stage 2: full-frame metadata and a split before waveforms/models

The current 495 metadata screen has at most 169 candidate TEST events, below 200 before any attrition. For a practical benchmark, propose expanding **metadata to the entire pinned 3,437-event CMT frame**, reusing all 495 accounted outcomes. At most 2,942 previously out-of-sample IDs would need new event metadata. Keep every frame label and known-zero/unknown-P status; do not filter source-frame geometry by pick success or waveform success. Preserve origin identity/date anomalies and the reviewed accepted-pick parser/clock. Cached failed/unknown cases are not silently refetched or relabelled under this design. No new metadata transfer cap is claimed: that acquisition needs its own prospectively frozen exact targets, deadlines, batch ledger and byte budget.

Build a 30-day/100-km connected-component **CMT-frame sequence screen** using all available frame origin time/geometry, including IDs not selected for waveforms; quarantine components crossing candidate 2015/2018 cuts and add a ±30-day boundary guard. Report 90-day/150-km sensitivity and independently documented major-sequence membership. Unknown geometry/time nodes cannot be treated as isolated. A graph on the 495 sampled IDs can miss bridges; even the full CMT graph is a catalogue heuristic, not proof of physical sequence independence from unsampled non-CMT earthquakes. Further targeted background-catalogue metadata would need a separately bounded scope if required for that stronger claim.

Candidate TRAIN 2003–2014 / DEV 2015–2017 / TEST 2018–2024 remains a proposal. Its pre-exclusion counts are 1,732/566/1,139, with TEST ceilings 49 Mw≥5.5, 19 Mw≥6 and 5 Mw≥7. Freeze the graph/guard rules, final cohort and acquisition lists before any model forecasts; do not move sequences or shift dates to rescue scores. Primary calendar transfer may reuse stations with that fact stated; a separately prespecified held-physical-station panel addresses station transfer. Do not impose a held-station requirement that silently destroys the primary tail sample.

## Stage 3: larger causal prefixes, then engineering controls

If frame metadata and the direct screen support the scope, acquire 10-second count windows for **all retained candidate calendar events**, rather than filling the sample until 200 successes appear. Maximum 3,437 events and 27,496 selected station targets at cap 8. Use the same per-deadline arrival ranking, sensor-family rule and absence handling as Stage 1; all failures remain in the frozen source denominator. TRAIN/DEV may be decoded for engineering audits; TEST acquisition/availability auditing is permitted, but forecasts stay sealed until a model/decision/metric freeze. Data-quality rules cannot be tuned to magnitude errors.

Proceed toward the proposed benchmark only if the common available-prefix TEST after sequence quarantine contains ≥200 events, ≥30 Mw≥5.5 and ≥15 Mw≥6. These are planning support floors, not power guarantees. The respective tail survival fractions need at least 30/49≈61% and 15/19≈79% even before sequence exclusions. One unusable largest event matters. Five potential Mw≥7 cases support case studies, not a precise aggregate conclusion. If the floors fail, retain the limitation; a revised split, additional region or reduced objective is a new prospective design, not a replacement draw or completed benchmark.

Train modest matched counts/late-metadata TEAM-LM and feature controls on the declared Mw population before the two mechanism candidates in the [shortlist](../geonet_method_shortlist/HANDOFF.md). Gaussian-mixture support remains unbounded through Mw8.0, with matched mean/median decisions and event weighting. Exact response/state/noise calibration is required for physical-invariance attribution, not every supervised engineering test. Archive picks describe retrospective annotations; none of these stages establishes online picking, packet delivery or operational warning latency.

## Byte estimates and archive route

These are explicit planning assumptions, not measured rates or approved caps. A 10-second triplet with 100–200 Hz native rate and 4-byte samples is 12–24 kB before miniSEED/container overhead. Use a deliberately conservative ×3 waveform overhead factor, with channel metadata 10–50 KiB per target and no cache savings. Higher rates, extra channels/epochs, over-delivery or poor compression can exceed these assumptions.

| Scope | Assumed response-body volume |
|---|---:|
| Draft response-only 288 targets, full response 100–500 KiB each | 28–141 MiB; no waveform evidence |
| Direct 96 / cap 8, worst 768 targets, channel metadata plus waveforms | approximately 34–91 MiB |
| Remaining 2,942 event QuakeML records at assumed 0.25 MiB/event | approximately 736 MiB; not a frozen allocation |
| Full 3,437 / cap 8, worst 27,496 targets, same channel+wave assumptions | approximately 1.18–3.16 GiB |

Stored float32 prefix arrays alone would be about 315–630 MiB at that full maximum; decoded float64 doubles this, and raw files/indices/masks/receipts add storage. Transfer-body budgets exclude HTTP/TLS overhead. Reuse may reduce metadata traffic; do not count that reduction before epoch/window joins are verified.

GeoNet documents archive waveform/station/event access, response detail levels, boundary over-delivery and batched selections. It directs large-volume waveform retrieval to its AWS Open Data route. Prefer bounded batched archive windows for the small screen; before a larger stage, compare those windows with an indexed S3 range approach without assuming full-day objects are cheap 10-second downloads. Do not copy entire daily waveforms or launch AWS merely because an open bucket exists. [Official FDSN documentation](https://www.geonet.org.nz/data/access/FDSN), [official AWS Open Data route](https://www.geonet.org.nz/data/access/aws).

GeoNet supplies data for research with attribution and dataset DOI citations. Publication should credit its waveform, station, earthquake and moment-tensor datasets. Open-data licensing does not verify AWS-credit eligibility or remove potential user-account compute/storage/transfer charges; paid infrastructure remains subject to the existing $2,000 eligible-credit ceiling and verified controls. [GeoNet data policy](https://www.geonet.org.nz/policy).
