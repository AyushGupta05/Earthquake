[P2] [P2] Propagate cancellations received during successful cleanup
run_diagnostic.py:302
If the child exits successfully and SIGTERM or SIGINT arrives during the final `process.poll()` or `process.wait()`, the handler records cancellation and cleanup deletes `COMPLETE.json`, but the pending `return code` still returns 0. Consequently, the CLI reports success for an invalidated diagnostic. The repeated-cancellation test starts with cancellation already recorded, so it does not cover this path. Defer returning until cleanup finishes and propagate any cancellation recorded during cleanup as a nonzero exit.
