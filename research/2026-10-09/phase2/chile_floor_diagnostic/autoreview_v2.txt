floor_math.py:40
Subtracting logsumexp from the original logits loses the normalization correction when the common offset is large. For accepted finite logits [1e20, 1e20, 1e20, 1e20, 1e20], logsumexp rounds to 1e20, so all normalized_weights become 1 instead of 0.2. With identical components, the reported density is five times too large and grad_logits sums to 4 instead of zero. These logits are representable as native float32 outputs. Subtract the row maximum first, then compute log-softmax entirely from the shifted logits.

run_diagnostic.py:281
The child is spawned before cancellation handlers are installed and before the cleanup try block begins.
