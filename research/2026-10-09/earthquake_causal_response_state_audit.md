# Causal response-state research audit

11 October 2026 (London). **No new learned or paid fit is justified by this audit.** Full causal instrument response and uncertain pre-P state remain a specific measurement hypothesis outside the completed scalar/FiLM and known-state covariance controls.

Invented arithmetic verifies a useful constraint: with the same observations, state uncertainty and noise model, an invertible causal canonicalization and a counts-space likelihood must give the same posterior. Coherent digital gain changes must also preserve it. An incorrect zero-state assumption can confound early amplitude, but these examples do not show that real high-magnitude errors have that cause.

Causal EEW response correction and sensor equalization already have close primary prior art, including [Anderson and Lees (2014)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/274245062_Instrument_Corrections_by_Time-Domain_Deconvolution). A bounded no-fit state-initialization falsification may be useful after actual raw-prefix and response availability is verified. Metadata correction cannot recover an unobserved delayed component or an instrument null.
