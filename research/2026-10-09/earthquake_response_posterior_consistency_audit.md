# Response-prefix posterior consistency: precise limits

The new source audit rejects unconditional equality between independently noisy sensor posteriors. Correctly modeled collocated instruments can produce different distributions from the same earthquake. A joint teacher can provide conventional forward-KL soft targets; this retains the uncertainty that reverse-KL matching can discard. Published SymVAE and standard distillation already overlap these principles.

Exact equality survives only for a verified information-preserving causal transformation with its matching boundary-state and noise law. A truncated delayed response can lose information that no inverse recreates. The invented Gaussian controls confirm these identities; they measure no earthquake error.

No new learned or paid fit is justified by this sketch alone. A specific early-response adaptation would still need admissible data and comparison against ordinary paired distillation, complete response/context inputs and causal canonicalization. This does not require independent physical calibration for every supervised engineering test; calibration and information-equivalence claims require the stronger evidence.
