# One practical innovation hypothesis

12 October2026. **Preferred: test an age-dependent, noise-referenced receiver-activity renewal likelihood.** It is a concrete new project experiment, not a verified novel algorithm or an improvement. A physical rupture-completion interpretation is expressly unsupported. Qualifying all65 native prefixes and sequence support is still root's prerequisite;17HTTP200 bodies are transport evidence, not usable model data. This document used no actual inputs, fits, AWS or Git.

The proposed defect is specific: a running-peak plateau conflates **continued excitation without a new maximum** and **a receiver signal that returns near its pre-P noise level**. The failed censored-peak model scores the former scalar statistic. A magnitude-conditioned activity-duration law instead scores runs of current excitation and quiescence, lets quiet signals reactivate, and retains right-censored exposure at a deadline. Whether that difference carries Mw information is unknown. It does not add information beyond raw samples already available to a full-prefix model; its hypothesis is more useful finite-data estimation of that information.

The conditional operator is specified in `PROTOCOL.md`: one early density followed by one likelihood factor for each newly observed activity block, conditioned on the initial prefix and preceding activity states. Age-dependent active-to-quiet and quiet-to-active transitions distinguish sustained activity from a flat peak and an isolated quiet gap. The operator never multiplies overlapping1/3/5 posteriors or reuses the first prefix as independent evidence. The one-second model also receives its first activity transitions; it is not a frozen one-second predictor that cannot possibly improve at1s.

## Closest primary prior art and novelty boundary

**Trugman et al.2019** already derive time-dependent peak-displacement saturation, introduce survival of the peak-reaching time under rupture variability, and compute Bayesian magnitude uncertainty. Their observation is evolving displacement peak and their physical saturation relation, rather than noise-level active/quiet renewal runs. Generic survival correction and broader high-M tails are therefore occupied. [Author-institution primary PDF](https://www.its.caltech.edu/~pagem/EEW_PGD_Saturation.pdf), DOI10.1029/2018JB017093.

**Münchmeyer et al.2022** already describe probabilistic rupture evolution and estimate magnitude densities from partial source functions/teleseismic waveforms. Their source results do not certify local receiver quiescence as a rupture endpoint or guarantee first-second discrimination. Calling our two receiver states stop/grow would overstate identification and duplicate this prior art. [Primary preprint v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2203.08622v1).

**Beyreuther and Wassermann2011** already use hidden semi-Markov models for seismic classification; explicit duration states are established. **Perron et al.2017/2018** already address noise/phase/coda windowing and the noise sensitivity of full-record energy endpoints. These exclude generic duration-aware states or signal/noise endpoints as new ingredients. [HSMM primary journal](https://npg.copernicus.org/articles/18/81/2011/), [windowing primary journal](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10518-017-0131-9).

A primary indexed GeoNet repeating-earthquake study by **Hughes et al.2021** defines a coda endpoint using an SNR drop below a threshold that persists for a fixed interval. That is close observable-definition prior art, though its task is waveform pairing/catalogue construction. [Primary article, indexed text only in this pass](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1029/2021GC009670). Conventional coda-duration magnitudes and amplitude-duration procedures further make a broad duration-magnitude claim untenable; no inaccessible formula is needed here.

**Only the narrow hypothesis survives:** an observed-state, nonabsorbing renewal likelihood conditioned on a strictly earlier prefix, normalized into an uncapped Mw density under exact1/3/5 network deadlines, may exploit run-age information more reliably than (i) a full-prefix density, (ii) direct use of exactly the same activity measurements and (iii) a memoryless free-hazard likelihood. The accessed sources do not verify that exact combination as novel. Surviving those controls would establish this project's useful operator contribution; a broader primary-art search and independent evaluation would still be needed before claiming publication novelty.

The project already rejected generic rupture survival, future-peak growth, tied peak censoring, posterior-revision and score changes. Their negative results remain constraints, not proof this differently measured activity process cannot work. The candidate must beat a current full-prefix/static-metadata model, rather than a stale first-second density or peak-only straw baseline.

## What8s can and cannot establish

`[T0-3,T0+5)` supplies a pre-event noise estimate and up to5s of local post-P activity, less when the selected station arrives late. It is sufficient to measure episode fragments, observed transitions and deadline censoring. It is **insufficient** to label final receiver-coda duration, physical source duration, unknown future asperities or a long-duration hazard tail. The pilot estimates only transitions over its observed age range; it performs no duration extrapolation beyond5s.

If all usable TRAIN episodes remain active through5s, a termination hazard fit is not earned: it can collapse toward zero without identifying run-age effects. Freeze and stop after that negative diagnostic. Do not automatically acquire longer records. A separate prospectively selected TRAIN-only extension would be necessary only for a subsequent hypothesis about durations beyond5s; longer observations cannot be supplied at deployment deadlines, and long source completion would still require independently meaningful state labels/observation physics. More data are not a rescue of the original frozen pilot.
