This audit read public protocols/research and primary online texts only: no actual waveform/metadata/prediction, processor edit, fit, cloud or Git action. It does not modify any frozen experiment.

## Exact mathematical boundary

These are deductions from the frozen operator, not empirical findings. The candidate is an observed, duration-dependent binary transition model conditional on magnitude and initial information. It need not be called a hidden-state model. Its run age is measured from the chosen threshold representation, not identified rupture age. Quiet-to-active can reflect S arrival, coda, propagation, noise, instrument response or renewed source radiation; final Mw labels cannot distinguish those causes.

A censored active run contributes observed stay factors; a measured exit adds its exit factor. Administrative termination at a deadline adds no exit. An incomplete quarter-second cell or an unarrived station is unavailable, not quiet. Finite cells resolve changes only at the observation discretization; they do not observe a physical stop instant. This is ordinary discrete survival/sequence likelihood, not a new censoring principle.

For immutable initial information I=(z,c,b1), and J_h complete cells,

`q_h(m) ∝ pi(m|I) product_{j=2..J_h} p(b_j|b_<j,m,I)`.

Therefore `q_5 ∝ q_3 product_{j=J_3+1..J_5} p(b_j|b_<j,m,I)`, including the normalizers. Conditioning later factors on initial information is coherent; each observation appears once. Reencoding later waveforms into a new initial prior/context, recomputing noise/normalization from future samples, or multiplying full-prefix posteriors breaks this stated filtration. Elapsed-time covariates may enter the new-cell factor, but a changed base prior needs its own observation model. An uninformative new factor leaves the posterior unchanged; surprising activity can increase entropy on a particular event.

**Name H accurately:** setting eta_on=eta_off=0 removes run-age dependence but retains gamma_s log(1+u_j). H is therefore a run-age-free, time-inhomogeneous transition control, not generally a geometric or memoryless run-duration model. A constant exit hazard gives the geometric special case. Keep the frozen H unchanged; C–H tests age dependence beyond global post-P time and other conditioning.

Even receiver-law parameter interpretation is limited: few exits, magnitude/context confounding and correlation of global time with run age can make coefficients weakly estimated. A positive transition-count gate is not identifiability or sufficient power. Mixture components need not acquire physically meaningful names. The lossy binary representation cannot have more information than the same underlying full waveform; a benefit would concern inductive bias, robustness or estimation with finite data.

## What a defensible favorable result could say

Conditional wording only: *At fixed early deadlines, an observed run-age-dependent receiver-activity likelihood, retaining censored exposure and activity resumption, improved magnitude-density and high-magnitude prediction relative to matched direct-feature and run-age-free controls on the specified evaluation cohort.* A reused small DEV pilot would support that cohort-specific statement only. It would not establish first use, causal rupture inference, natural-population bulk preservation, or published superiority. Search absence is not priority evidence.

The differentiating hypothesis is useful factorization and run-age dependence on a prospectively fixed observation space. Threshold9, quarter-second cells, Bayes normalization, Gaussian mixtures, censoring and generic reactivation are not individually claimed new. The fixed50 extension adds no new Mw>=6 identities, so it cannot solve independent extreme-tail support merely by expanding TRAIN.

## Controls that would disprove or narrow it

- C failing H rejects the proposed extra run-age mechanism; tying F credits ordinary activity features rather than the likelihood structure. Keep equal observations/capacity and disclosed joint-versus-conditional objective differences.
- A magnitude-independent transition control or past-only whole-event label permutation retaining the apparent gain would weaken evidence that transitions supply magnitude information. These are later prospectively fixed diagnostics, not extra current fits or DEV-selected controls.
- Poor held-TRAIN transition calibration/censored-run prediction, or unstable likelihood ratios across groups, would reject the claimed observation-law adequacy even if a point estimate moved upward.
- Tail improvement with worse whole MAE/MedAE, lower-Mw errors, proper scores or worst error fails the user objective; a one-second failure prevents an all-1/3/5 claim. No median-action substitution or selected seed/horizon.
- Gains disappearing under independently held events/stations, native family/rate/gain/noise-floor strata, or arrival/telemetry clock controls would narrow a physics/transfer claim. A floor at one count squared means the measurement is not automatically invariant to changing digital gain when the floor activates.
- Identical initial information/activity histories must give identical candidate posteriors even if invisible future rupture histories differ. Replayed/overlapping cells must not sharpen the density twice; direct and incremental calculations must agree, with no appended censor exit and independent numerical integration checks.

No additional acquisition, fitting, control arm, threshold/window change, or source edit is authorized by this recommendation. Current implementation and tests are other tasks; this audit inspected neither their private inputs nor outcomes.
