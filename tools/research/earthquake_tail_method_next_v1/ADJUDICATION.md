# Tail method decision, 12 October 2026

**No genuinely distinct, scientifically supported new mechanism is earned by the present evidence. The next useful actual model run is one bounded native-count information diagnostic after the fixed65 support/sequence qualification.** It is an engineering baseline, not a new winning method. Its precise protocol is in `PILOT_PROTOCOL.md`. No data, waveform, inventory, prediction, checkpoint, resource, fit or Git operation was used for this document.

The strongest conditional method remains **distinct, compatible-Mw real-event transfer with target bulk replay**. Its extra information is new labelled recorded earthquakes, not more repetitions of the existing rare examples. TEAM-LM already investigated cross-region transfer, sparse large-event training and fine-tuning: transfer helped some regions, while fine-tuning could damage their largest-event estimates. Its regional labels and evaluation times differ from GeoNet Mw and our exact1/3/5 clock. Those findings justify a hypothesis, not a matched result here. [TEAM-LM v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2101.02010v2), §§2.1,3.3,4.3–4.4.

I specifically reject calling **bulk-protected donor-gradient transfer** a new mechanism. For bulk gradient `g_b`, donor gradient `g_d`, the proposed conflicting-gradient replacement

`g_d' = g_d - min(0, <g_d,g_b>)/||g_b||² * g_b`

is the established projection used by PCGrad. Protecting replay gradients asymmetrically changes an experimental policy, not the core mechanism. Moreover `<g_b,g_d'> >= 0` only gives a local linearized loss statement; it gives no held-event bulk MAE, median or worst-error guarantee after a finite optimizer step. We have not measured destructive interference on a qualified Mw cohort. Do not add another optimizer grid. [Yu et al., NeurIPS2020](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2020/file/3fe78a8acf5fda99de95303940a2420c-Paper.pdf).

The completed growth, censored-innovation, revision, score, rare-exposure, response, geometry and polarization failures remain failures under their fixed tests. Fourier/phase representation, source/nuisance disentangling, survival, historical retrieval, coherent gain transformations and risk wrappers already have close prior art. Native counts plus nominal channel descriptions do not supply a new physical observation law. The recorded source-scaling obstruction and identical-onset counterexample also rule out treating stretched recordings as newly observed high-Mw evidence. This conclusion is based on the named project reports; it is not an exhaustive proof that no new method can exist.

## Conditional transfer experiment, only after cohort qualification

Use one unchanged amplitude-preserving TEAM-style encoder and uncapped five-Gaussian Mw density in every arm. The loss, updates, parameter count, target replay batches, final-checkpoint rule, preprocessing, available stations, two seeds and deadline exposure are identical. Half of each training macro-batch is a fixed target natural-event replay stream. Only its other half changes:

| Arm | Other half of macro-batch | Question |
|---|---|---|
| A | Ordinary target TRAIN | Equal-compute target control |
| B | Repeated target TRAIN Mw>=5.5, matching D's supported bands | Does event diversity beat repeating rare labels? |
| C | Ordinary compatible-Mw donor events, matched donor region/sensor strata/count | Does rare-source information beat ordinary donor diversity? |
| D | Distinct compatible-Mw donor TRAIN events Mw>=5.5 | Real-event diversity candidate; established transfer mechanism |

Use density NLL averaged within each half and then across halves, not unreported magnitude weighting. Select the final fixed update, with a TRAIN-only calibration checkpoint as a prespecified diagnostic. Target population calibration must be measured; changed exposure does not automatically preserve its posterior. Do not bolt on a new prior correction to rescue a failed arm. If target TRAIN has no labels in a donor band, B cannot isolate diversity from range extension there; explicitly report the combined intervention. Freeze donor eligibility and exact event/sequence exclusions before either cohort's evaluation. Donor full-response equivalence is needed for physical invariance claims; ordinary count-domain transfer still needs an explicit units/sensor/domain declaration and matched engineering controls.

Hypothesis: D reduces target Mw>=5.5 fixed-mean action MAE beyond A/B/C at every1/3/5 deadline, while preserving natural-event bulk and worst errors. **Novelty hypothesis: none accepted. Performance hypothesis: untested.** The Mw eligibility restriction and guards are protocol choices, not a new algorithm.

## Independent promotion criteria

Before fitting the larger study, freeze a genuinely independent target evaluation cohort, sequence groups, all denominators, inference cost, two seeds and all comparisons. Registered200usable/30Mw>=5.5/15Mw>=6 floors remain minimum support, not power guarantees. Mw>=7 is descriptive unless independently supported.

For each seed, use the density mean as the primary point decision. At each horizon require D's event-macro tail MAE gain>=0.02 magnitude against every A/B/C; both seeds must agree. Simultaneous one-sided95% paired sequence-block intervals must support that gain and zero-margin nondegradation of natural-event bulk MAE, MedAE, CVaR95, NLL and CRPS. Predeclare resampling and multiplicity across comparisons/deadlines/guards. Do not resample the two seeds as independent earthquake observations or switch to an ensemble action. Report>=6 separately; inconclusive subgroup evidence cannot become a>=6 claim.

Report P95, maximum absolute error, signed high-Mw bias, every large-event error, PIT and50/80/95% coverage. Any observed maximum-error worsening in either seed/horizon vetoes the stated worst-error promotion. Interval crossing a boundary is inconclusive. All failed or missing predictions retain their fixed denominator; report coverage and failure cost separately. Full-population NLL/CRPS are proper scores; scores conditioned on the realized high-Mw label are descriptive tail diagnostics and cannot alone certify a proper target posterior. Small1-second apparent gains, wider distributions or a favorable median action cannot substitute for the fixed objective.

**Recommendation to root:** finish qualification, then freeze/run the six-fit information baseline if its stated support gate holds. Do not spend on an invented mechanism or PCGrad add-on. Keep compatible-Mw real-event transfer as the only subsequent conditional method, honestly attributed as established prior art. No tested novel winner exists now.
