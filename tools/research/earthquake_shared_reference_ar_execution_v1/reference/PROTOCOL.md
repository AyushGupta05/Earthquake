## One measurement-law hypothesis

Keep current shared-innovation S unchanged: its conditional mean adds the same empirical joint support vector nu to every innovation. That is a normalized coherent working model. It is not automatically the law for an error in one reference denominator shared by all observed levels.

L instead assumes latent component level z_jc obeys the existing affine AR law and the observed normalized log-power mark is y_jc=z_jc+u_c with one fixed reference offset across the prefix. With first history exactly zero, substitution yields

    y_1c = alpha_1c + beta_1c(M-mref) + u_c + epsilon_1c
    y_jc = alpha_jc + beta_jc(M-mref)
           + rho_c*y_(j-1,c) + (1-rho_c)*u_c + epsilon_jc, j>1.

alpha/beta/sigma/time covariate, diagonal rho, prior and empirical12 support remain the same. Only d_1c=1 and d_jc=1-rho_c replace S's all-one loading. The rho derivative is y_previous-u in later factors; no detach, learned loading or new coefficient is permitted. Call this AR-consistent loading under the stated shared-level model, not a verified physical error law for these records. Additive waveform noise, fitted-center error, floor/quantization, gain/path/site effects, temporally correlated residuals and rupture duration remain unmodeled mechanisms.

For fixed support ell, multiply each new cell/component Gaussian once; marginalize ell outside the entire product with unchanged weight1/12, combine with the same five-Gaussian static prior and normalize over all-real magnitude. ## Claim boundary and follow-up evidence

This is a single predeclared correction/ablation, not a new generic Bayesian method. The AR loading follows standard measurement-error/random-effect algebra; existing EEW already has Bayesian early-amplitude inference and evolutionary distributions, including [Cua & Heaton2007](https://www.its.caltech.edu/~heatont/papers/VSmethod_cua_heaton%20%282%29.pdf), [Meier et al.2015](https://doi.org/10.1785/0120150098), [2015 GJI multi-event Bayesian EEW](https://academic.oup.com/gji/article/200/2/791/610068), and [Zollo et al.2023](https://doi.org/10.1029/2022EA002657). A primary indexed [2022 Bayesian EEW paper](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/12/24/12849), Eq.13, explicitly carries a previous posterior into the next prior; [Lancieri & Zollo2008](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1029/2007JB005386) already describes evolving magnitude PDFs from early P/S displacement peaks. Neither1->3->5 Bayesian updating nor shared measurement uncertainty alone is claimed novel. Prior-art scope/verification limits and receiver-versus-rupture/noise-support caveats are inherited explicitly from the frozen public shared_reference_ar_audit_v1 research note.
