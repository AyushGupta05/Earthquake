## One comparator and its scientific status

The reference is Trugman et al.2019, [Peak Ground Displacement Saturates Exactly When Expected](https://www.its.caltech.edu/~pagem/EEW_PGD_Saturation.pdf), DOI10.1029/2018JB017093. Their work uses a time-dependent amplitude likelihood and a magnitude prior; early amplitude saturation can leave substantial uncertainty. Their calibrated, filtered, distance-corrected displacement and station aggregation are different from our native-count inputs. This proposal borrows only the likelihood structure and the importance of observation age. It is an empirical low-capacity baseline, not their physical saturation-model reproduction or a proposed novelty.

For each existing common-cohort TRAIN event and unchanged1/3/5 cutoff, retain the exact original T0/P and literal components. Use the already frozen pre-T0 mean and twelve quarter-second noise-power cells. Let N_c=sqrt(max(1,median(cell powers))) for channel c. From centered native counts on [selectedP,T0+h), form A_c=max absolute count amplitude. Use the channel with greatest A_c/N_c, ties by the frozen literal component order; selection uses only the current prefix. The observed scalar is y=log10(max(A_c,1count)/N_c), the known floor is L=-log10(N_c), and u=(T0+h-selectedP) in seconds. No future quality test, integration/filter/response removal, source distance, inferred origin feature or completed-rupture duration enters this feature. Original local-P age >=.25seconds is required. The same pre-T0 centering/noise applies across all three horizons; no horizon-dependent demeaning.

Fit ONE shared model: latent y*=a+b*m+c*log10(u)+epsilon, epsilon Gaussian(0,sigma²), b,c>=0 and sigma>=0.1 in log10 amplitude units. Four fitted parameters only. For A_c>1count, use the Gaussian density at y; for A_c<=1count use Gaussian CDF at L. This is ordinary left censoring at an explicitly numerical amplitude floor, not a claim of ADC clipping or rupture termination. The dimensionless count/noise statistic still mixes path, sensor frequency response and noise environment; it is not displacement, and the count floor breaks exact gain invariance when active. A linear magnitude term does not solve physical saturation; this deliberately simple comparison tests whether lower estimation variance yields useful held-group information at all.

Use one Gaussian magnitude prior whose mean/std are estimated exclusively from the current fitting TRAIN groups, with std floor0.5 magnitude units. Equal operational-group weight, equal event weight within group; labels/types are unchanged. This prior describes this deliberately selected TRAIN sample, not natural Gutenberg-Richter prevalence. The prior-only posterior is the fixed diagnostic control. Normalize p(m|y,u) proportional to p(y|m,u)*p0(m) over the real line with certified quadrature error, retaining uncapped mean/median, NLL and CRPS. At each horizon start from the same p0 and its single current cumulative-amplitude likelihood. Never multiply overlapping1/3/5 likelihoods or posteriors as independent observations.
