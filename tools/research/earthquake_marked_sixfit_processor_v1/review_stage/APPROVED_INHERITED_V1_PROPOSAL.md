## Concrete question and practical staging

Does a low-dimensional continuous native-quarter likelihood, conditional on the SAME uncertain pre-T0 reference through the whole prefix, improve rare-high-Mw prediction without damaging bulk or worst errors? The information hypothesis is continuous three-component marks versus binary max-power activity. The more specific uncertainty hypothesis is shared finite noise sensitivity versus plug-in, independently redrawn, correlated-Gaussian and robust residual alternatives. These are separate questions; a win over a small direct model alone proves neither.

Stage0 is earned-source/numerical/representation/used-capacity feasibility, TRAIN only. Stage1 has exactly three small arms S(shared Gaussian marks), P(plug-in same Gaussian marks), D(direct summary posterior), each seeds20261012/20261013: six new fits, in seed-major S/P/D order. All use identical architecture, initial parameter bytes, TRAIN scalers, labels/event orders and300updates. Strong unchanged full-waveform B/F and renewal H/T are required external comparators; reuse requires parent independent rebound of original roster/native input, exact forward checkpoint and proper-score truth. Missing or failed comparators make the result incomplete, never a reduced-denominator success.

Stage1 is an exploratory screen, not the full scientific claim. Only if S passes every original24 comparison and both P/D comparison guards AND all source/score gates pass may ROOT consider Stage2. Stage2 is fixed in advance here: I(per-cell independently redrawn joint finite noise), T(time/magnitude-time replacing AR/censor history), G(ordinary correlated Gaussian component residual), R(ordinary plug-in Student-t residual, fixed df5), same two seeds: eight additional fits. S/P/D weights are retained unchanged, never retrained or selected by DEV; every declared Stage2 comparator is evaluated with the same strict guard. Source/runtime/numerical implementations for Stage2 must be frozen before its fits; no alternate model may be substituted if one is infeasible. A Stage1 failure ends this study as a negative/incomplete screen, without altered epochs, thresholds, arm deletion or DEV-based architecture rescue. No special shared-noise claim until all four Stage2 controls are completed and survive the fixed comparison. An unavailable numerical control remains a stated limitation, not a solved alternative.

This staging caps initial training at six small fits. It does not promise a feasible full14-fit study or a positive result. The full-waveform comparators have about5007/5147 parameters and are separate stronger comparators, never called capacity-matched to the small models. Reuse means no extra optimizer step; rescoring still requires its independently reviewed fixed distribution scorer.
