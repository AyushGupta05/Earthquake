[P2] Separate predictive stop rules from falsification of the observation model
RESEARCH_NOTE.md:74
Lack of benefit from the corrected loading, or regression in bulk/worst-error metrics, does not falsify a shared denominator-error mechanism. For example, at rho=0 the two loadings are identical even when shared reference error is physically present; an improved nuisance support can then provide the only benefit. More generally, the explicitly uncalibrated delete-one support and other model misspecification can mask a correct loading's value. Keep these criteria as reasons to stop development or reject a predictive-improvement claim, and reserve physical-model falsification for controlled checks that isolate its assumptions.
