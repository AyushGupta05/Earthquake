## What the primary sources establish

The original SeisBench preprint describes GEOFON as global regional/teleseismic recordings: 2,270 events, 275,274 traces, magnitude approximately 2–9, mainly M5–7, and smaller regional events concentrated in Europe/northern Chile. Table 1 lists variable lengths and 20–200 Hz. Its overall counts are **not Mw-only, independent-sequence or usable-window counts**. Iquique is a separate small local picking dataset; its 409-event total supplies no qualified Mw tail count. The paper's GPLv3 statement concerns SeisBench software, not a demonstrated dataset license. [Woollam et al., arXiv v1, GEOFON/Iquique descriptions, Table 1, §6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2111.00786).

The rendered GEOFON converter declares ZNE velocity-sensor **counts**, response not restituted, and loads an inventory without response. It writes preferred-magnitude value/type/author/uncertainty; event IDs use the resource identifier's last eleven characters. Trace fields include network/station/location/channel prefix, sensitivity, absolute start, sample rate and phase-specific sample/status. Its default slice spans sixty seconds before the earliest included pick through sixty after the latest. It merges, attempts rotation, converts to an array, and records completeness/spikes. Origin-time splits use 1 November 2012 and 15 March 2013 boundaries. These are **documented converter fields/operations**, not verified columns, calibrations or bytes of an immutable precompiled release. [Official implementation, rendered revision e5616ad33](https://seisbench.readthedocs.io/en/main/_modules/seisbench/data/geofon.html).

The separately rendered array utility initializes missing samples to zero, orders multiple pieces by length with an overlap-completeness warning, and subtracts each resulting full-array component mean. Rotation failures can be ignored. **Inference:** direct prefix cropping is not automatically a causal or gap-preserving physical-amplitude input. A prefix-only recentering cancels a constant full-window mean in exact arithmetic, so this observation alone does not prove an irreparable leak; rounding, fills, rotations and the exact released pipeline still need verification. Do not interpret zero padding as recorded motion or a scalar sensitivity as a full response. [Official utility, rendered revision 11d251b59](https://seisbench.readthedocs.io/en/main/_modules/seisbench/util/trace_ops.html).

The two source pages render different revisions; attempts to retrieve both files at the GEOFON page's abbreviated revision failed. That failure establishes **no immutable source/release match**, not absent implementation or data. Documentation also differs on the initial year: historical benchmark/paper descriptions say 2009–2013, while the converter docstring says 2010–2013. No release checksum, complete provenance chain or usable-field census was obtained. The historical benchmark page describes Iquique as picked 2014 aftershocks; a large mainshock mentioned in a dataset description is not a magnitude label for every example. [Official historical benchmark descriptions](https://seisbench.readthedocs.io/en/stable/pages/data/benchmark_datasets.html).

GEOFON's provider describes a global service using partner networks, automatic/manual revisions and a separate moment-tensor stage fitting three-component long-period waveforms. Its retrospective labels and picks do not establish receiver-time availability at a 1/3/5-second deadline. A documented moment-tensor procedure does not certify that every preferred label in the SeisBench release is an independently anchored Mw. [Provider processing description](https://geofon.gfz.de/eqinfo/howitworks/).
