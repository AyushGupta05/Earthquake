# Prospective V4 contract recommendation

These are advisory schema requirements consistent with root's separate pinned [normative V4 proposal](../geonet_retained_failure_qualification_proposal_v4/PROPOSAL.md). No existing source/schema is changed. The NEW implementation contract must freeze its exact protocol/study identity and reject older identities rather than accept aliases. Proposed additional field names below are not yet an implemented schema.

Retain the existing closed plan fields for exact integer `precontext_s:3` (reject bool/float/string/null/omission), exact limits, source mappings, original signed-nanosecond clocks and four ordered parent slots. Each slot keeps its original role/selection and literal common network/station/nonblank two-character location/channel prefix. Component order is E,N,Z; no expected metadata rate is invented. Before any reader, validate all 12 distinct canonical body references and the complete plan/authorization/provenance graph.

Each component input additionally binds a finite `input_class` (`transport_qualified_candidate` or `retained_transport_failure`), the exact parent-pinned source collection/audit/returned-summary and member receipt references, and body byte/hash custody. The classification is derived from independently audited receipts, not HTTP status alone or a caller's unchecked boolean. All 12 cached body references remain in the private custody inventory; an error-body hash may be verified as opaque bytes but no scientific reader receives it. Original receipt/status provenance remains available to root; no error text/XML parsing is needed for the science decision.

Every private component output retains its input class, finite decode status, eligibility state/reason, literal identity/rate/representation evidence, original clock relations and exactly three window cells. Proposed finite statuses are `not_decoded_transport_failure`, `unsupported`, and `decoded`. Proposed eligibility reasons are `eligible`, `transport_unqualified`, `decode_unsupported`, `identity_unverified`, `rate_unverified`, and `representation_unverified`. Freeze deterministic precedence for simultaneous eligibility failures. Noneligible cells have `available:null`, `mask:unknown`, their fixed eligibility reason and no asserted core false; retain supported decoded summaries privately where present. Do not silently discard evidence or choose a different channel.

Eligible cells preserve the unchanged core primitives. Their public-facing reason is `qualified`, `core_incomplete`, or `core_unknown`, with `available:true/false/null` and `mask:available/incomplete/unknown`. Each cell fixes the original left/deadline and strict catalogue cutoff. A failed core predicate may be known-false only when expected literal identity, supported consistent observed rational rate and valid decoded representation are already established. Declared structural eligibility checks may inspect the object; cropped amplitude/finite checks may inspect only that horizon.

Each of the 12 joint cells retains `{left_ns,deadline_ns,available,mask,reason,common_observed_rate,lattices_compatible}`. Keep finite reasons `qualified`, `component_incomplete`, `component_unknown`, `mixed_component_rates`, `common_lattice_mismatch` and the priority table in DESIGN.md. Rate is null unless all three eligible component cells are true and share an exact observed rational rate; lattice is null unless that rate condition is met. Parent/supervisor independently recomputes the joint states and counts from retained validated primitives. Summary replay does not independently re-decode amplitudes or certify their finiteness.

Public aggregate retains four-slot denominators for E/N/Z at each horizon and joint cells at each horizon, partitioned into available/incomplete/unknown; separate all-four catalogue-pick counts and availability intersections. Include finite input/decode/eligibility counts that do not conflate transport success with scientific eligibility. Every component/horizon and joint/horizon remains present. No public IDs, NSLC, keys, paths, timestamps, amplitudes, exception messages, private receipts or body hashes. Counts must be exact nonnegative integers and preserve fixed totals.

Keep `raw_record_timing_verified=false`, `raw_record_segmentation_verified=false`, and false flags for filename partition, metadata rate, physical orientation/units/clock, response/state adequacy, clipping, calibrated three-component motion, operational availability, model-input qualification and model improvement. No old version's all-pass or true/false outcome is overwritten.

Keep proposed inherited hard numerical controls: 12 objects; 67,108,864 bytes per object; 805,306,368 total bytes; 16,000,000 advertised samples per object; 192,000,000 total; 120-second supervised envelope; 8,388,608 stdout bytes; 131,072 stderr bytes. No hard RSS guarantee. Body/hash/source/runtime/schema, any cap, deadline or termination failures abort the invocation and cannot be promoted as complete. Unsupported, custody-valid scientific inputs retain unknown cells and may continue within limits. Normal return plus a hash-verified complete receipt and confirmed group termination remain mandatory.
