The narrow renewal comparison remains unfinished. The following primary texts were rechecked through the web tool while the processing deadline failure was preserved. They constrain hypotheses rather than supply matched numerical superiority evidence.

- Zhang et al.,2026, **A2MAG**, DOI10.1038/s44304-026-00242-3: current publisher early-release abstract describes first3-second K-NET acceleration and systematic underestimation aboveM6. The indexed abstract reports0.33MAE; this is not our Mw/native-count/event/clock benchmark. The currently rendered page is principally the abstract, not a fully verified architecture/release. [Publisher](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44304-026-00242-3).
- Zhang et al.,2024, **Universal neural networks**, DOI10.1038/s43247-024-01718-8: full text includes a1D magnitudePDF, normalized waveform plus amplitude factor and epicentral distance, and evolving station averages. Its large-event example shows limitations before rupture is fully observed. A Gaussian outputPDF, amplitude bypass and progressive updating are established prior art. Its location-conditioned network and elapsed-time clock cannot provide a directly matched comparator score for our receiver-counts task. [Publisher](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43247-024-01718-8).
- Zhu et al.,2024, **DHLnet**, DOI10.1029/2023EA003363: full text groups magnitude knowledge into amplitude,energy,distance,frequency features and fuses these with learned features. This supports a strong ordinary continuous-feature control; continuous energy/amplitude information alone is no new method. Multi-station averaging and reported independent-region behavior require matched geometry, clocks, labels and inputs before comparison. [Publisher](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1029/2023EA003363).
- Lyu et al.,2025, **WaveCastNet**, DOI10.1038/s41467-025-65435-2: full text predicts wavefields, rather than magnitude, and evaluates finite-fault extrapolation. Its limited early input can underestimate larger-event amplitudes; longer windows help. That motivates censor-aware growth modeling but does not establish1/3/5-second magnitude gains. The text also identifies finite-fault diversity and synthetic-to-real gaps. [Publisher](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-65435-2).
