# Primary-source recheck before receiver-renewal fits

Checked 13 October 2026. No candidate fits or candidate feature values were read for these notes. The frozen V2 comparison stays unchanged. This is a targeted primary-source addendum, not an exhaustive novelty certificate. No published benchmark is matched by the present reused DEV pilot.

Wang, Li and Song (2020) already combine early-period and distance-corrected displacement estimates to reduce large-event underestimation while keeping small-event estimates stable. Their method extends selected time windows and changes filtering for low-SNR records. The study uses predominantly surface-wave magnitudes, near-source records and network aggregation; its reported validation errors therefore cannot be compared directly with our single-receiver typed-Mw task. It is close prior art for selective tail correction and evolving windows. Current V2 instead tests a conditional distribution from observed activity transitions and duration, with a matched calendar-time control. This mechanistic difference needs empirical evidence and does not itself establish novelty. [Primary full text](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-78046-2).

The authors’ TEAM repository implements magnitude/location estimation and PGA prediction as separate tasks, exposes a five-Gaussian magnitude mixture and provides waveform/pooling baselines. Distribution output and a CNN/transformer architecture are already established. Our borrowed design choices must be described as baselines; a candidate claim must concern an independently tested update mechanism beyond equivalent inputs and capacity. [Official source and publication references](https://github.com/yetinam/TEAM).

EEWNet’s author abstract describes direct vertical-accelerogram magnitude estimation and an architecture that adapts to input duration. This abstract supports including raw-waveform baselines; it does not supply a matched high-Mw error table, split or calibration comparison. Only the abstract was rechecked here, not the full paper. [Author abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.05531).
