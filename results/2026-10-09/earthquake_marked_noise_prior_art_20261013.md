The candidate hypothesis is narrower: learn an all-real magnitude distribution from continuously observed native quarter powers while preserving a single joint uncertainty state for the pre-earthquake noise reference through successive prefixes. Compare it with the same plug-in likelihood, direct summary posterior and the previously frozen stronger waveform models. Each new cell contributes once; initial precontext excludes post-P measurements. A separate all-positive feasibility pilot is prospectively frozen before feature values, retaining the entire common population or failing admission. The full censored and correlated/robust controls remain additional prerequisites for a mechanism claim.

Neither analytic inference nor invented examples establish novelty or improved earthquake error. The pilot must protect lower-magnitude and whole-event error, worst errors and proper distribution scores at every duration and both seeds. Its reused exploratory DEV set cannot establish population bulk preservation or published-benchmark superiority. Independent representative, magnitude/clock/split-matched evidence remains necessary.
