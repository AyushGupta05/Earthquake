# Next GeoNet data and benchmark plan

**The fixed 96-event direct waveform screen launched and stopped incomplete after its first waveform body timed out.** Its 412/417 attempts and 419 private artifacts remain preserved; no retry, resume, replacement or successful-denominator promotion has occurred. A separate configured-order archive study then transferred four fixed TRAIN/DEV vertical objects. Offline 1/3/5 identity and sample-grid qualification is pending. A NEW eight-horizontal-object transport source protocol is being prepared conditionally for those same four selections, without changing the failed96 study. Response inventories alone cannot establish continuous usable prefixes.

The frozen original allocation included all 60 pinned Mw≥6 events, two events from each of the 15 smaller-magnitude/calendar cells, and six events in [5.5,6). Fixed hash selection precedes availability outcomes; failures are never replaced. Its intended downstream protocol was: at each of 1/3/5 seconds, use up to eight earliest accepted arrived stations. The union through five seconds is download bookkeeping; future station identities and picks cannot enter earlier predictions. Every selected event remains in the denominator, including those without queryable picks.

Native-count prefixes can support ordinary supervised engineering controls with audited timestamps, channels and declared preprocessing. Calibrated physical motion and invariance claims require stronger response evidence. A failed response request must not be interpreted as an absent waveform. The earlier six-event audit left waveforms unattempted for two response-204 targets, so their raw availability remains unresolved.

The 495-event metadata sample is too small to become the proposed benchmark: at most 169 candidate TEST events before attrition. A larger plan would first obtain remaining full-frame geometry, including zero/unknown-P events and events not selected for waveforms, then freeze a CMT-frame sequence screen and the retained calendar cohorts before predictions. Even a full CMT sequence graph is a catalogue heuristic; non-CMT bridges can remain unresolved.

The candidate TEST ceiling is 1,139 events, including 49 Mw≥5.5, 19 Mw≥6 and five Mw≥7. Planning floors of 200 usable events, 30 Mw≥5.5 and 15 Mw≥6 are support floors, not power guarantees. The largest five support case studies. Collect a fixed cohort with retained failures, rather than continuing until enough successes appear. No fit is earned by these plans or the four-object transport success; the larger independent benchmark and current metadata thresholds remain unresolved.

Under explicit 100–200 Hz, three-component and metadata-overhead assumptions, the direct screen is estimated at 34–91 MiB of response bodies; a full cap-eight cohort is roughly 1.18–3.16 GiB. These are planning estimates, not measured rates or operative caps. GeoNet documents batched archive access, boundary over-delivery and an AWS Open Data route for larger access. [Official FDSN documentation](https://www.geonet.org.nz/data/access/FDSN), [AWS archive documentation](https://www.geonet.org.nz/data/access/aws).
